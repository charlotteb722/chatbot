Free public support is real competition for introductory AI help. The opening is practical, hands-on help with a specific task, which free courses do not provide.

## Positioning gaps

1. **Price transparency.** None of the local providers found publish prices in search results. Clear starting prices would stand out.
2. **Ownership and no lock-in.** Lock-in is a known fear. Few providers make client ownership a headline.
3. **Business analysis first.** Agencies talk about design and "getting online". Nobody found talks about understanding how the business works before building.
4. **Websites plus practical AI and automation.** Rare combination locally. It supports repeat work after the site is built.
5. **Crieff and Strathearn presence.** Little dedicated local competition in search results.

## Differentiation statement (recommendation, for copy in Phase 3)

A Crieff-based consultant who starts with how your business works, builds a website you own outright, tells you exactly what it costs to run each year, and can help you use AI and automation to save time afterwards.

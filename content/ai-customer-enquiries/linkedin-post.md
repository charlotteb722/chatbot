How many times this week have you answered the same customer question?

Opening hours. Delivery areas. Prices. Changing a booking. None of it is hard, but it arrives at awkward times and each reply takes a few minutes to get right.

AI can help with this, though probably not in the way people expect. The useful part isn't a clever chatbot. It's much simpler:

1. Write down the five questions you're asked most often.
2. Write a clear, accurate answer to each.
3. When an enquiry comes in, give an AI assistant your answers and the customer's message, and ask it to draft a reply using only that information.
4. Read it, adjust it, send it.

The important instruction is "if the information doesn't cover their question, say so". That stops it guessing at a price or a policy you don't have.

You're still the one replying. You've just saved yourself the blank page.

If you'd like to try it, start with step one this week. Five questions, five answers.

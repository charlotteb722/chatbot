# Prompt card: drafting replies to customer enquiries

*Draft for Charlotte's review*

## Before you start

Write a short answer to each of your most common questions. Include prices, timings, what's included, and what the customer should do next. Keep it in one document and update it when anything changes.

## The prompt

> Here is information about my business:
> [paste your answers document]
>
> A customer has sent this message:
> [paste the message]
>
> Draft a friendly, short reply using only the information above. Use British English. If the information doesn't fully cover their question, say which part is missing and suggest I reply personally.

## Before you send

- Is every fact in the reply correct and current?
- Does it sound like you?
- Did it make up anything that isn't in your document?
- Is this something that needs your personal attention instead, such as a complaint, refund or sensitive situation?

## Keep it safe

Avoid pasting in personal details you don't need, like full addresses, payment information or health details. Remove them from the customer's message first if they aren't relevant to the reply.

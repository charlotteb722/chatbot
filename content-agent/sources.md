# Source rules

Edit this file whenever you like. The agent reads it at the start of every command.

## Prioritise (official documentation and primary sources)

- **AI vendors:** OpenAI (openai.com, help.openai.com, platform.openai.com/docs), Anthropic (anthropic.com, support.claude.com, docs.claude.com), Microsoft (learn.microsoft.com, support.microsoft.com, Microsoft 365 blog), Google (blog.google, support.google.com, workspace.google.com)
- **UK government and regulators:** gov.uk (including DSIT and the AI Security Institute), ICO (ico.org.uk), ONS (ons.gov.uk), CMA, ASA/CAP for advertising rules
- **UK business organisations:** Federation of Small Businesses (fsb.org.uk), British Chambers of Commerce, British Business Bank, Chartered Institute of Marketing, Be the Business
- **Platforms (for social and marketing features):** official help centres and newsrooms of LinkedIn, Meta, Canva, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, WordPress

## Acceptable as secondary (use to find the primary source, not as the only support)

BBC News, the Guardian, Financial Times, Reuters, The Verge, TechCrunch, Ars Technica, Computer Weekly

## Avoid

- Unverified social media claims (posts on X, LinkedIn, TikTok, Reddit) unless they come from the vendor's official account and are confirmed on its site
- Promotional articles and listicles without evidence ("10 AI tools that will 10x your business")
- Affiliate review sites
- Undated pages, and anything about AI products more than 12 months old that can't be rechecked against the current official page

## Rules

- A fact counts as verified only if the agent opened the page and read it there.
- Statistics need the original publisher.
- Prices and features change often: always record the date the page was accessed.

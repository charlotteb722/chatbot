# Brand settings

Edit this file whenever you like. The agent reads it at the start of every command. Your `charlotte-style` skill covers voice in detail. This file covers what's specific to the content pack.

## Who it's for

UK small business owners, sole traders and entrepreneurs. Capable in their own field, often new to AI, short on time, careful with money. Explain only the technical detail they need to do the task or make a decision.

## Subjects

- Practical AI tools for small businesses
- AI marketing and content creation
- Business automation and productivity
- Website development and digital business support
- Practical examples of how businesses save time with AI

## Tone

Professional, friendly and practical. British English. Warm, direct and specific, like a real person explaining something useful. Start from a practical problem, show the steps, and end with one thing the reader can do next.

## What the content is for

To show practical expertise and invite genuine enquiries. Every post gives the reader something they can use. The selling is light: the usefulness does the work.

## Calls to action

Use word for word. At most one per post, and only where it fits naturally.

1. Would you like help using AI in your business? Send me a message.
2. Discover how I can help your business at https://charlottebjuren.com/
3. Want to save time with AI and automation? Get in touch for a chat.

## Platform guidance

| Platform | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 120-220 words | Short paragraphs. Open with the problem, not "I'm excited to share". No more than 3 hashtags. |
| Facebook | 60-140 words | Conversational, local-business feel. A question can work well. |
| Instagram | 60-120 words, plus 5-8 hashtags | First line must work alone. Points to the carousel where relevant. |
| Carousel | 6-8 slides, under 30 words per slide | One idea per slide. Slide 1 is the hook, last slide is the next step. |
| Blog (optional) | 600-800 words | Problem, what's changed, worked example, steps, cautions, next action. |

## Never

- Invent experiences, client results, testimonials, qualifications, prices or availability
- Use hype or filler: "game-changer", "revolutionise", "unlock", "supercharge", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "harness the power", "the future is here"
- Use em dashes, semicolons or ellipses in post copy
- Use clickbait or fear ("AI will replace your business")
- Mention employers, colleagues or client names

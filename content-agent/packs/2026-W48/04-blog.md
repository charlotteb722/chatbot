If your business already pays for Microsoft 365, there's a good chance you have an AI assistant you haven't opened yet. Before you think about paying for anything extra, it's worth trying what's already there on a few real tasks.

This guide explains what Copilot Chat is, what's included and what's a paid extra, and three simple things to try this week. It's based on Microsoft's own documentation, checked in November 2026 (sources at the end).

## What is Copilot Chat?

Copilot Chat is Microsoft's AI assistant for work. Microsoft describes it as a web-grounded AI assistant included at no additional cost with eligible Microsoft 365 plans [F1]. If you don't have a paid Copilot licence, Microsoft says you can still try Copilot Chat by signing in with your work account [F2].

"Web-grounded" means it mainly answers from information on the web. It doesn't automatically look through your emails and files. Microsoft says it can use your own content, but only when you provide it with your request [F5]: by pasting it in, uploading a file or choosing a file [F8].

You can find it in a few places: the Microsoft Copilot app (on the web, desktop and mobile), the Edge browser, and inside Outlook and Teams [F6].

## Is it safe to use for business?

When you sign in with your Microsoft work account, Microsoft says Copilot Chat is covered by its Enterprise Data Protection. Your prompts and responses are protected by the same contractual terms and commitments that apply to your Exchange email and SharePoint files [F10].

That's reassuring, but good habits still matter. Only share the information a task actually needs, and avoid pasting in more personal details about customers or staff than necessary.

## Three things to try this week

Pick tasks from your normal week, so you can judge whether it genuinely helps.

**1. Summarise a web page.** Open a supplier's or competitor's page in Edge and ask Copilot Chat for a summary. Microsoft says it can summarise website content and some document types shown in Edge [F7]. Try asking: "Summarise this page in five bullet points for a small business owner."

**2. Get the key points from a long document.** Upload a document you'd normally read in full, such as a supplier contract or a guidance note, and ask for the main points [F8]. Then check those points against the original before you rely on them.

**3. Draft a reply to a customer email.** With an email open in Outlook, ask Copilot Chat to draft a reply. Microsoft says that in Outlook and Teams it can use the content you have open, without you copying and pasting [F8]. Give it a clear instruction, such as "Draft a friendly reply confirming Thursday at 10am, under 100 words", then edit the draft before sending.

## What's included, and what's a paid extra?

This is where people often get confused, so here's the simple version.

**Included with eligible Microsoft 365 plans (Copilot Chat):**
- AI chat that mainly uses the web, with your own content when you provide it [F5]
- Copilot Chat in Outlook, Teams, Edge and the Copilot app [F1, F6]
- Simple agents that work from instructions and public websites [F11]

**With the paid Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on:**
- Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Teams [F3]. Without the add-on, Copilot Chat users don't get Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OneNote [F9]
- Microsoft's pre-built agents, such as Researcher and Analyst [F3]

Microsoft also notes that some features are subject to fair use limits [F5], and that more advanced AI work is billed as you use it [F4]. Microsoft lists the add-on as available for organisations with up to 300 users on plans including Business Basic, Business Standard and Business Premium [F12].

## Is the paid add-on worth it?

Try this simple test after a week with Copilot Chat:

1. Write down the tasks you do each week **inside** Word, Excel, PowerPoint or Teams.
2. Next to each one, note whether AI help there would genuinely save you effort.
3. If the list is short, Copilot Chat may be all you need for now. If it's long, the add-on may be worth looking at properly.

The aim isn't to have every AI feature. It's to use the ones that make a real difference to your week.

## Your next step

This week, sign in to Copilot Chat with your work account and try one of the three tasks above on something real. Keep a note of what worked and what didn't. That note will tell you more than any sales page.

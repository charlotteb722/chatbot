## LinkedIn post

**Topic:** Pause, check, report. Simple habits against AI-powered scams

The scam emails that used to give themselves away with clumsy spelling are getting harder to spot.

The ICO warns that criminals are using AI to make attacks faster and harder to detect [F1]. That includes convincing, personalised messages that impersonate colleagues, clients or trusted suppliers [F2], and even AI-generated voices and video used to trick staff [F3].

Three habits worth agreeing with your team this week:
1. **Pause.** Don't click links, open attachments or share bank details if you're not sure a message is genuine [F10]
2. **Check.** My suggestion: confirm any change of bank details or urgent payment request by phoning a number you already have, never one in the email
3. **Report.** Forward suspicious emails to report@phishing.gov.uk [F11]

The ICO also recommends multi-factor authentication on email [F5], and GOV.UK offers free staff training that takes less than 30 minutes [F13].

Would you like help using AI in your business? Send me a message.

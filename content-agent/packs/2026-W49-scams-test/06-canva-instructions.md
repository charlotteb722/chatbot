# Canva and posting instructions: 2026-W49-scams-test

Everything here is manual. Nothing has been posted. Copy the text from `05-publication-copy.md`.

## 1. Before you design: checks only you can do

- [ ] **The Cyber Advisor offer.** GOV.UK says the free 30-minute session is "currently" available. Check the GOV.UK cyber security guidance page before posting if it's more than a few weeks after 22 November 2026.
- [ ] **"It takes two minutes"** in the Facebook post is an everyday estimate, not a sourced claim. Keep it or delete it.

## 2. Set up in Canva

- **Brand:** your usual colours and fonts. One accent colour for "Pause, Check, Report" (carousel slides 4-6).
- **Sizes:** Carousel **1080 × 1350 px**, 8 pages · LinkedIn **1200 × 627 px** · Facebook **1080 × 1080 px**
- **Avoid:** hooded-hacker or skull imagery, and real company logos or real-looking bank emails.

## 3. Carousel: one design, 8 pages

| Page | Headline | Icon idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scam emails are getting harder to spot | Magnifying glass over an envelope with a sparkle |
| 2 | What's changed | Two speech bubbles, one with a mask |
| 3 | Not just email | Phone and video camera icons |
| 4 | Pause | Pause symbol |
| 5 | Check | Phone with a tick |
| 6 | Report | Envelope with an arrow |
| 7 | Lock the door | Padlock with a shield |
| 8 | Want a safer setup? | Arrow, website in bold |

**Export:** PNG for Instagram (all pages, one carousel), PDF Standard for LinkedIn (document post).

## 2. Set up in Canva

- **Brand:** your usual colours and fonts. Two accent colours for the carousel: one for "what's new" (slides 2-4) and one for the steps (slides 5-7).
- **Sizes:** check them against Canva's presets.
  - Carousel: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait)**, 8 pages, for Instagram and LinkedIn
  - LinkedIn image: **1200 × 627 px**
  - Facebook image: **1080 × 1080 px**
- **Avoid:** the OpenAI or ChatGPT logo and ChatGPT screenshots. Use simple icons instead.

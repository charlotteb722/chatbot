## Blog article

**Title:** What does ChatGPT say about your business? A guide for UK small businesses

**Meta description:** OpenAI's Businesses in ChatGPT explained for UK small businesses: what's available, what's US-only, and a simple check you can do today.

When someone asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, your business might come up in the answer. Or it might not. Either way, it's worth knowing what it says about you, because OpenAI puts its weekly users at 1.2 billion people.

In November OpenAI added a new way for businesses to take part. Here's what it is, what UK businesses can and can't use at the time of writing (22 November 2026), and the practical check every business can do today.

## What OpenAI has launched

On 10 November 2026 OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Through Business Hub, a business can claim its listing, manage its information and see insights about how people engage with it in ChatGPT. Those insights include the number of mentions and website clicks a business receives. OpenAI says it doesn't share users' conversations with businesses, and the insights are aggregated.

There's also a newer feature called Business Agents, which lets eligible businesses set up an agent that answers customer questions. For now, OpenAI says these are available to eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States.

## Can a UK business claim its profile?

It depends. OpenAI's announcement says any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim its business, and the UK is on OpenAI's list of supported countries for ChatGPT. But its help page adds that availability depends on the business's location and type, and that eligibility depends on factors including industry, location and verification checks.

One line matters for many small businesses. OpenAI's help page says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing. So if you run a local shop, café or salon in the UK, claiming may not be open to you yet.

If you'd like to see what's offered to your business, sign in to ChatGPT, open Business Hub from the sidebar and search for your business by name. Be ready for checks. OpenAI says you'll need identity verification with Persona before you can publish changes or view insights, which may involve a government-issued ID, business registration information and proof that you can represent the business.

Claiming isn't a shortcut to being recommended, either. OpenAI says claiming a profile doesn't change its visibility in ChatGPT.

## The check every UK business can do today

You don't need Business Hub for this part. ChatGPT may show information about your business whether or not you've set anything up, so start by finding out what it says.

1. **Ask.** Open ChatGPT and ask it to describe your business: what you do, who you help, where you work and how to contact you.
2. **Check.** Compare the answer with reality. Note what's right, what's missing and what's out of date. Try a second question that a customer might ask, such as "Who offers [your service] in [your town]?"
3. **Fix it at source.** OpenAI says ChatGPT may use other sources when answering questions about a business, and that updates can take time to appear. So update the places you control, starting with your website. Make sure it clearly says what you offer, where and for whom, with current contact details.
4. **Recheck in a few weeks.** Changes won't show straight away.

Here's an illustration. It's a made-up example, not a real business. A mobile dog groomer asks ChatGPT about their business and finds it lists an old town they no longer cover. They update the "Areas we cover" section on their website and their other public listings, then check again a few weeks later.

## A few cautions

- **Not everything is available here yet.** Don't plan around Business Agents if you're in the UK, because OpenAI lists them as US only for now.
- **Keep it accurate.** If you do claim your business, OpenAI's terms say you must keep the information current and correct anything inaccurate or outdated.
- **No promises.** Nothing here guarantees that ChatGPT will mention or recommend your business. What you can do is make sure the information about you is clear and correct.

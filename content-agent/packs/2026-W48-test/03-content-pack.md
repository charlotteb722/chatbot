**Topic:** Businesses in ChatGPT, and a quick check any UK business can do

OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week [F2]. So it's worth knowing what it says about your business.

On 10 November OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Through Business Hub, a business can claim its listing, update its details and see insights such as mentions and website clicks [F1, F5].

For UK businesses, access is mixed at the time of writing. OpenAI says availability depends on the business's location and type [F6]. Its help page says local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing [F9], and Business Agents are US only for now [F4].

Note anything wrong or missing, then fix it at source, starting with your own website. OpenAI says ChatGPT may also use other sources when answering questions about a business [F10], so clear, current information on your site is the part you control.

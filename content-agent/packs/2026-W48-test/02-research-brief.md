## Summary

On 10 November 2026 OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta. Through Business Hub, a business can claim its listing, update its details and see how often it's mentioned. For UK businesses, access is uncertain. OpenAI says eligible businesses where ChatGPT is available can claim, but availability depends on location and type, local stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing US listing, and Business Agents are US-only. Any UK business can do the practical part today: ask ChatGPT what it says about you, then fix the information at source, starting with your own website.

## Assumptions and interpretation

- **A1.** Every UK business can do the "ask ChatGPT about your business" check today, because ChatGPT is available in the UK (F15) and may mention a business whether or not it has claimed a profile (F12).
- **A2.** Whether a particular UK business can claim its profile isn't confirmed. OpenAI says it depends on location, type and checks (F3, F6, F13), and the help page excludes local stores and restaurants outside the US for now (F9). So the content must not tell UK readers they can claim. It can only suggest they look in Business Hub to see whether the option is available to them.
- **A3.** Because ChatGPT may use other sources (F10), a clear, current website and consistent public listings are the part a small business controls. This is advice, not an OpenAI claim.
- **A4.** Business Agents (F4) should be mentioned only as "US-only for now", not recommended.
- **A5.** No claim about time saved, sales or traffic should be made.

## Suggested practical takeaway

Open ChatGPT and ask it to describe your business, what you offer and where you're based. Note anything wrong or missing, then fix it at source: your website first. If you'd like to see whether you can claim your profile, open Business Hub from the ChatGPT sidebar and search for your business (F7). Availability varies (F6, F9).

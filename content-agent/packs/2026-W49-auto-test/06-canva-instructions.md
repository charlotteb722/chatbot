## 1. Before you design: checks only you can do

- [ ] **Your privacy page.** If you publish this, readers may look at how *you* handle data requests. Check your own website has a clear contact point for them.
- [ ] **Legal advice wording.** The blog says it's general guidance, not legal advice. Add the same line to the LinkedIn post if you'd like.

## 2. Set up in Canva

- **Brand:** your usual colours and fonts. One calm accent colour for the step slides (3-7). Avoid red or warning-style colours.
- **Sizes:** check them against Canva's presets.
  - Carousel: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait)**, 8 pages
  - LinkedIn image: **1200 × 627 px**
  - Facebook image: **1080 × 1080 px**
- **Avoid:** the ICO logo or anything that looks official or government-branded.

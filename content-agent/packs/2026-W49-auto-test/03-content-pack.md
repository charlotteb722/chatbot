## LinkedIn post

**Topic:** AI-written subject access requests, and a calm way to handle them

Imagine an email lands that's three pages long, quotes laws you've never heard of and asks for "all data held". It may well have been written with AI. It still counts.

On 11 November the ICO published guidance on subject access requests drafted with AI [F1]. Its message is clear: a request isn't invalid just because AI helped write it, and you must treat it like any other [F2].

What that means for a small business:
1. The usual deadline applies: as soon as possible and within one month [F4]
2. Long or confusing doesn't automatically mean "complex", so don't assume you get extra time [F5]
3. You can ask for clarification if you genuinely need it, and the clock pauses while you wait [F6]
4. You only need a reasonable and proportionate search [F7]
5. Watch for fakes. The ICO warns AI can be used to create false ID or letters of authority [F8]

One thing to do this week: make sure everyone who answers your emails, phone or social media knows a request can arrive through any of them [F12].

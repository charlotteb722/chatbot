## 1. Before you design: ten-minute account check

Google's help pages describe these features for **Gmail on a computer**. The posts follow those pages, but nobody has checked them in a live UK account yet. Please do this first. If anything doesn't match, tell me before posting and I'll adjust the copy.

**Free personal Gmail (UK)**
- [ ] Open a long thread. Can you see "Summarize this email" at the top? Note the exact spelling on your screen ("Summarize" or "Summarise") and use that spelling in all the copy.
- [ ] If not, check both settings are on: "Smart features in Gmail, Chat, and Meet" and "Smart features in Google Workspace".
- [ ] In a thread with a task and a due date, do you see "Remind me"? The posts say "you may see", so it's fine if it only appears sometimes.
- [ ] When writing an email, confirm that Help me write and the prompt bar **don't** appear. If they do, the posts' "not available on free Gmail in the UK" line must change.

**Google Workspace (UK)**
- [ ] Do summaries appear on a long thread?
- [ ] When replying, is there a prompt bar at the bottom, or a Help me write button? Do Formalize, Friendly and Shorten appear after you click Create?
- [ ] If nothing appears, your admin may have switched Gemini off, or the rollout may not have reached the account yet.

Record the result in the notes column of `content-agent/packs/log.csv`.

## 2. Set up in Canva

- **Brand:** use your usual brand colours and fonts throughout. Pick two accent colours: one for "Both accounts" slides and one for "Google Workspace" slides.
- **Sizes:** these are common sizes. Check them against the presets Canva offers when you create a design.
  - Carousel: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait)**. One design with 8 pages works for both Instagram and LinkedIn.
  - LinkedIn image: **1200 × 627 px** (or reuse carousel slide 1)
  - Facebook image: **1080 × 1080 px**
- **Avoid:** Gmail screenshots and Google logos. Use simple icons from Canva's library instead.
